# Opportunity backlog

Keep product experiments separate from current implementation commitments. Revisit at playback, queue, and retention milestones, not after every small change.

| Opportunity | Smallest experiment | Proposed success signal | Revisit when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Return Ticket | One durable queue/position snapshot, Explore Temporarily, Return | At least 6 of 10 pilot listeners use it on 3 separate days without prompting | Persistent queue and reliable seeking exist |
| Listening Spaces | Three manually named queue/position contexts; only one active engine | Listeners voluntarily resume at least two contexts across multiple days | Return Ticket shows repeated use |
| Listening Postcards | Manually publish ten 3-5-track pages with personal notes and ordinary provider links | Recipients open multiple tracks, share their own, or request app import | Core retention and existing recommendation behavior are observed |

These are utility/retention/sharing hypotheses, not claims of novelty or guaranteed growth. Competitor references: Pear Desktop and Spotify Jam. Do not add automatic classification, cloud sync, hosted audio, or a social network to the current scope.
